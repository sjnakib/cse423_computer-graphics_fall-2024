# Study Materials

These are the notes and practice files I kept from CSE423 Computer Graphics, Fall 2024, BRAC University.

## Contents

### Course outline

- `course-outline/cse423-course-outline-summer-2022.docx`: the course outline. This copy is from Summer 2022. I kept it as a reference for the course topics.
- `course-outline/cse423-post-midterm-topics.pdf`: the list of topics for the post-midterm part of the course. It covers transformations, projections, color models, illumination and shading, and curves and surfaces.

### Notes

- `notes/my-class-notes.pdf`: my handwritten class notes, 22 pages.
- `notes/shakir-rouf/`: lecture notes by Mr. Shakir Rouf. These are not my notes. I kept them as a reference. There are six PDFs, covering lectures 1-2 through lecture 7.

Mr. Shakir Rouf is a faculty member at BRAC University. His profile: https://cse.bracu.ac.bd/faculty_profile/201/mr_shakir_rouf

### Practice sheets

- `practice-sheets/cse423-final-suggestion.pdf`: a 3-page set of practice questions for the final exam. The questions cover transformations, projections, lighting, and color models.

## Textbooks

The textbook PDFs are not included in this repository because they are copyrighted. The books I used were:

- *Computer Graphics: Principles and Practice*, 2nd edition, by James D. Foley, Andries van Dam, Steven K. Feiner, and John F. Hughes. ISBN-13: 978-0201848403.
- *Computer Graphics: Principles and Practice*, 3rd edition, by John F. Hughes and others. I only had a sample of this edition.

The lecture slides are also not included. They are course material from the instructor and are kept outside this repository.
